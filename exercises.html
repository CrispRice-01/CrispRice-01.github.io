Common Beginner Exercises

Learning basic exercises can make it easier to create your own workouts. Start with controlled movements and focus on proper technique before increasing weight or repetitions.

Squats

Squats primarily train the legs and glutes. Keep your chest lifted and push your hips back while lowering your body.

Push-Ups

Push-ups strengthen the chest, shoulders, arms, and core. Beginners can perform them from the knees if needed.

Lunges

Lunges strengthen the legs while also challenging balance and coordination.

Planks

Planks strengthen the core. Keep your body in a straight line while holding the position.

Glute Bridges

Glute bridges strengthen the hips and glutes and can easily be performed at home.

Calf Raises

Calf raises strengthen the lower legs and can help improve ankle stability.
