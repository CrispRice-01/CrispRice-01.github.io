Beginner Workouts

Starting a workout routine does not need to be complicated. Beginners can focus on basic movements that train the major muscle groups while gradually building strength and confidence.

Types of Workouts

Upper Body

Upper-body workouts can include push-ups, rows, shoulder presses, and other simple arm exercises. Beginners should focus on controlled movements and comfortable resistance.

Lower Body

Squats, lunges, calf raises, and glute bridges are beginner-friendly exercises that can help strengthen the legs and hips.

Core

Planks and other basic core exercises can help develop stability and support many other movements used during exercise.

Simple Full-Body Workout

Here is an example of a simple workout a beginner could use as a starting point.

Squats: 2 sets of 10 repetitions

Push-Ups: 2 sets of 5-10 repetitions

Glute Bridges: 2 sets of 10 repetitions

Plank: 2 sets of 20 seconds

Walking: 10-20 minutes

Tips for Beginners

  • Start with manageable workouts.
  • Focus on learning proper exercise technique.
  • Take breaks when you need them.
  • Include rest days in your routine.
  • Gradually increase your workouts over time.
