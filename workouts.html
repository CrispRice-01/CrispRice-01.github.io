Beginner Workouts

Beginner workouts should focus on learning proper movement patterns and gradually building strength. You do not need complicated equipment or extremely long workouts to get started.

A short routine completed consistently can be more helpful than starting with a difficult routine that becomes hard to maintain.

Simple Full-Body Workout

Bodyweight Squats

Complete 3 sets of 10 repetitions.

Push-Ups

Complete 3 sets of 5–10 repetitions.

Walking Lunges

Complete 2 sets of 10 steps per leg.

Plank

Hold the position for 20–30 seconds.
