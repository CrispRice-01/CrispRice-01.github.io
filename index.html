Getting started with fitness does not have to be complicated. Bris' Fitness is designed to help beginners understand the basics of exercise, running, recovery, and creating a consistent routine.

Fitness Made Simple

Fitness information can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially for someone who is just getting started. This website organizes beginner fitness information into simple sections so that you can focus on building healthy habits one step at a time.

Strength Training

Learn beginner-friendly exercises and simple workouts that can help you gradually improve your strength.

Running

Recovery

Learn why sleep, hydration, rest days, and other recovery habits are important parts of a balanced fitness routine.

Build a Routine That Works for You

You do not have to completely change your lifestyle overnight. Starting with manageable goals can make fitness feel less intimidating. As you become more comfortable, you can gradually increase your activity and develop a routine that fits your schedule.
