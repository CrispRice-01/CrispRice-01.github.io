Start Your Fitness Journey

Getting started with fitness does not have to be complicated. Bris' Fitness is designed to help beginners understand the basics of exercise, running, recovery, and creating a consistent routine.

Get Started

Beginner Workouts

A beginner workout should focus on learning basic movements and gradually becoming stronger. You do not need complicated equipment or extremely long workouts to get started.

Upper Body

Try exercises such as push-ups, shoulder presses, rows, and light arm exercises.

Lower Body

Squats, lunges, glute bridges, and calf raises are simple exercises for developing lower-body strength.

Full Body

Combine upper-body, lower-body, and core exercises for a balanced beginner workout.

Running Tips

New runners should focus on consistency instead of speed. Starting with a combination of walking and jogging can make running easier and more enjoyable.

  • Begin with short distances.
  • Warm up before you run.
  • Wear comfortable running shoes.
  • Take walking breaks when needed.
  • Increase your distance gradually.

Common Exercises

Squats

Squats primarily train your legs and glutes while also requiring core stability.

Push-Ups

Push-ups strengthen the chest, shoulders, arms, and core. Beginners can modify them by using an elevated surface.

Planks

Planks are a simple exercise for developing core strength and stability.

Recovery

Recovery is an important part of any fitness routine. Your body needs time to rest after exercise so that you can return to your next workout feeling prepared.

  • Get enough sleep.
  • Drink water throughout the day.
  • Include rest days in your routine.
  • Eat balanced meals.
  • Pay attention to how your body feels.

Example Weekly Routine

Monday: Full-body strength workout

Tuesday: Walking or light running

Wednesday: Rest and recovery

Thursday: Full-body strength workout

Friday: Running or cardio

Saturday: Light activity or stretching

Sunday: Rest

Keep It Simple

You do not have to completely change your lifestyle overnight. Start with manageable goals, stay consistent, and gradually build a routine that works for you.
