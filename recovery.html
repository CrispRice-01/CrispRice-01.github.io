Recovery and Rest

Exercise is only one part of a balanced fitness routine. Recovery gives your body time to rest between workouts and prepares you for future activity. Beginners should remember that taking time to recover is part of the process.

Recovery Basics

Sleep

Maintaining a consistent sleep routine can help you feel prepared for exercise and everyday activities.

Hydration

Drinking water throughout the day is an important part of supporting your normal daily activity and exercise routine.

Rest Days

Rest days provide a break from harder workouts. Light activities such as an easy walk can also be included when appropriate.

Example Weekly Fitness Routine

A weekly routine can combine strength training, cardiovascular activity, lighter movement, and rest. The example below shows one simple way a beginner might organize a week.

Example Beginner Weekly Routine
Day Activity Focus
Monday Full-Body Workout Strength
Tuesday Walking or Light Running Cardio
Wednesday Rest Recovery
Thursday Full-Body Workout Strength
Friday Walking or Running Cardio
Saturday Light Activity Active Recovery
Sunday Rest Recovery

Recovery Tips

  • Include rest days in your weekly routine.
  • Drink water throughout the day.
  • Try to maintain a consistent sleep schedule.
  • Use light activity when you want a less demanding day.
  • Avoid increasing your training too quickly.
